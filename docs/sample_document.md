# Project Aegis - Orbital Station Specifications

This document outlines the engineering specifications, environmental controls, and operational limits of the **Project Aegis Orbital Station**.

---

## 1. Physical Dimensions & Orbit
*   **Orbit Class:** Low Earth Orbit (LEO)
*   **Altitude:** 420 kilometers above sea level
*   **Primary Structural Material:** Titanium-carbide matrix alloy
*   **Total Mass:** 340 metric tons
*   **Pressurized Volume:** 910 cubic meters

---

## 2. Power Generation & Storage
The station relies entirely on high-efficiency photovoltaic collectors and integrated battery systems.
*   **Solar Array Capacity:** 1.2 Gigawatts (GW) of continuous generation at peak solar exposure.
*   **Solar Cells:** Gallium-arsenide multi-junction arrays.
*   **Energy Storage:** 4,200 Kilowatt-hour (kWh) lithium-sulfur battery buffer.
*   **Primary Distribution Grid:** 120-volt DC loop with redundant step-down nodes.

---

## 3. Life Support & Habitat Limits
*   **Crew Capacity:** 24 crew members (nominal operation), 45 (maximum surge limit).
*   **Primary Atmosphere:** 78% Nitrogen, 21% Oxygen, 1% Argon at 101.3 kPa pressure.
*   **Water Reclamation Rate:** 98.4% efficiency using multi-stage osmotic filtration.
*   **Main Cooling Loop:** Liquid ammonia heat pipe radiators.
*   **Solar Shielding Dome Height:** 4.5 meters (located in the botanical recreation module).

---

## 4. Emergency Protocols
In the event of a depressurization alert or thermal control loop failure, the station activates the following emergency procedures:
1.  **Isolation:** Blast doors automatically seal the damaged module within 1.8 seconds of sensor breach detection.
2.  **Rerouting:** Life support nodes redirect oxygen reserves through the backup supply conduits.
3.  **Evacuation:** Crew members assemble at Docking Port Charlie within 6 minutes to board escape pods.
